Mode

Manual — your 6 channel remote drives it, or the pad below when the remote is off. Auto — it follows the row, stops at each plant and decides. Move any stick on the remote and it drops straight back to manual.

0%

How many rows it does, how long a row may be and how far apart the rows are all live in Settings under Deciding.

The spray boom

Five nozzles 140 mm apart across a 700 mm boom, in three sections. It lays a band down as it drives — a boom that sprays while standing still dumps the lot in one spot, so the rover shuts it off if it stops. The valves pulse about ten times a second to hit the rate you set; check the label on the bottle for the rate and for how long before you can pick.

Drive it yourself

throttle 0% · steer 0%

Up and down is throttle, left and right points the front wheels. This machine steers like a car: it cannot turn on the spot. W A S D or the arrow keys work too. Works in Manual, and only when a hand remote is off — the remote always wins.

Soil probe

A lead screw drives the spike about 65 mm into the soil, waits for the reading to settle, then pulls it out again. A moisture reading taken in the air only tells you the air is dry.

Positions come from the rover's own odometry, so without wheel encoders they drift over a long row. Everything it has flagged

These are kept in the rover's own database, so they survive a power cut. The Messages panel on the Live page only shows what has happened since it was last switched on.

Patrols

A patrol only starts if the rover is parked, not stopped, has more than 40% battery and the remote is off. If any of those is not true it says so in the messages and waits for the next one. Spraying stays off unless you tick it — and the pump still has to be armed.
