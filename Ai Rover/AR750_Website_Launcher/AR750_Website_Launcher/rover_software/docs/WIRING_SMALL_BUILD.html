2L298N aur motors

L298N ek side par sirf 2A tak sambhalta hai. Ek side par 2 motor lagenge to board garam hoga. Shuru mein speed 30% rakhiye aur board ko chhoo kar dekhte rahiye. Zyada garam ho to baad mein har side ke liye alag L298N ya BTS7960 lagaiye. Software dono ke saath chalta hai.

3Raspberry Pi 4 ke pins

Pi ko aise rakhiye ki USB aur Ethernet ports neeche hon aur 40 pins ki line right side mein. Pin 1 sabse upar, andar wali line mein hai. Uska solder pad chaukor (square) hota hai. Pin 2 uske bagal mein, board ke kinare wali line mein.

Do-daant wala probe module ke doosre 2 pins par lagta hai. Probe ko halki geeli mitti mein daaliye aur neela screw tab tak ghumaiye jab tak DO wali LED jal na jaye. Wahi "geela" ki seema ban jayegi. VCC ko 5V par lagaya to DO pin Pi ko 5V bhejega aur Pi ka pin kharab ho jayega.

Pi AI Camera

Pi 4 mein camera ka ek hi port hai, isliye purana Camera v2 nikaal dijiye.

Cable: dono sire barabar chaude wali cable lagaiye. "Standard – Mini" wali cable, jiska ek sira patla hai, sirf Pi 5 ke liye hai.

Pi 4 par: "CAMERA" likha port HDMI aur audio jack ke beech hai. Kaala latch dheere se upar uthaiye, cable ke chamakte contacts HDMI ki taraf rakh kar andar daaliye, phir latch dabaiye.

Software AI Camera ke liye zaroori imx500-all package khud install karta hai.

6Jodne ka kram

  1. Sab band.Battery nikli ho, Pi band ho, aur rover ke pahiye zameen se upar ho (kisi dabbe par).
  2. Buck converter set kijiye.Har buck ke input par battery lagaiye, output par multimeter lagaiye, aur screw ghuma kar A ko 5.1V aur B ko 5.5V par laaiye. Phir battery hata dijiye.
  3. Camera cable.Upar bataye tareeke se, Pi band rakh kar.
  4. L298N ke signal.IN1 pin 32, IN2 pin 35, IN3 pin 33, IN4 pin 37, aur L298N GND se Pi ka pin 34.
  5. Motors.Left motor OUT1/OUT2 par, right motor OUT3/OUT4 par.
  6. Soil sensor.VCC pin 1, GND pin 9, DO pin 22.
  7. Servos.Orange taar table ke hisaab se Pi ke pins par. Laal aur brown servo rail par. Servo rail ka minus Pi ke pin 14 par.
  8. Power ki taar.Battery + → fuse → switch → L298N 12V, buck A IN+, buck B IN+. Battery − → L298N GND, buck A IN−, buck B IN−. Buck A ka USB-C Pi mein.
  9. Table se milaiye, phir battery.Har taar section 3 se ek baar milaiye. LiPo alarm lagaiye, battery jodiye, aur switch on kijiye.
