==============================================================================
  AR-750  -  THE WEBSITE, ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
==============================================================================

  One file.  Double click  AR-750.bat.

  That starts everything at once: it finds the rover on your wifi (even if
  its address changed), waits for it if it is still starting, opens it on
  this laptop, and opens a page with a QR code - scan it with the phone and
  the phone opens the same rover. Both show the live camera and both can
  drive it, the arm and the camera, at the same time.

  Press M in that window for the menu: the pretend rover for trying things
  on this laptop, sending new code to the Pi, the self test, and the rest.
  The menu tells you at the top whether Python is installed, whether it has
  been set up yet, and whether the console is running.

  Building the rover?  The wiring for the small build (Raspberry Pi 4,
  L298N, 3S LiPo, camera servo, 5-DOF arm, soil sensor, AI Camera) is in
  rover_software\docs\WIRING_SMALL_BUILD.html - open it in any browser.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE MENU
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1  OPEN THE CONSOLE ON THIS COMPUTER          <--  start here

     Runs the complete console on this computer with a pretend rover, and
     opens it in your browser. Everything works: the dashboard, the live
     map, the camera pane, missions, the plant history, the charts, the
     alerts, the recordings, the patrol timetable and every settings page.
     The numbers are made up, but the buttons are the real ones.

     Sign in the first time with       admin  /  agrirover

     It makes you change both straight away. After that, the username and
     the password you set are stored on the website itself. You change them
     on the Settings page whenever you like. You never edit any code to do
     it.

     The very first run downloads about 60 MB of Python libraries and takes
     a few minutes. It only ever does that once. After that it opens in
     seconds.

     It needs Python on this computer. If you have not got it, the menu says
     so and choice 3 still works. To install it, go to
     https://www.python.org/downloads/ and tick "Add python.exe to PATH" on
     the first screen of the installer - that tick box is the whole trick.

     If the console is already running, choice 1 just reopens the browser
     rather than starting a second copy.


  2  CONNECT TO THE REAL ROVER

     For when the rover is actually built and switched on. It asks for the
     rover's address the first time, remembers it in rover_address.txt, and
     opens it in your browser.

     The rover prints its own address on the screen when it starts,
     something like  192.168.1.42:8080. Your computer and the rover have to
     be on the same wifi.

     To reach it from outside the house, read
     rover_software\docs\REMOTE_ACCESS.md. Short version: use Tailscale, and
     do not open a port on your router.


  3  JUST LOOK AT THE DESIGN

     Opens a single saved page that shows what every screen looks like. No
     Python, no rover, no internet. Nothing on it is live and nothing is
     saved. Good for showing someone the design in ten seconds.


  4  CHECK EVERYTHING IS WORKING

     Starts a throwaway copy of the rover on its own port, with its own
     empty data folder and its own login, and then calls every page and
     presses every button on it: signing in, the live telemetry socket, the
     camera stream, the field map, the charts, the timetable, the settings,
     the emergency stop and the pages that must refuse you when you are
     signed out. It prints a pass/fail line for each, shuts the copy down and
     deletes it.

     Nothing you have set up is read or written - not your database, not your
     photos, not your password. The report is kept at
     rover_software\tests\last_selftest_report.txt

     Worth running after you change anything, and worth running once on the
     Pi when the rover is built.


  5  SEND THE CODE TO THE RASPBERRY PI

     There is no separate "Raspberry Pi version" to hunt for. The folder
     rover_software IS the rover's program - the same one you have been
     clicking through on this laptop, with the fake hardware swapped for the
     real hardware when it finds it.

     This choice does the whole job in one go: makes a clean copy, sends it
     to the Pi as ~/AR750_Rover, runs install.sh there, starts the rover,
     checks it answers from the Pi and from this laptop, remembers its
     address for choice 2, and runs the self test on the Pi. The Pi asks for
     its own password once, for the install - type it and press ENTER.

     It leaves out the Windows virtual environment, the caches and this
     laptop's data\ folder, so the Pi's own database, photos and password
     are never overwritten - send it again after every change and lose
     nothing. Everything it did is written to tools\last_pi_deploy.txt.


  6  LINK THIS LAPTOP TO THE RASPBERRY PI

     Needed once: the first time, and again after the SD card is flashed
     fresh. It finds the Pi on your wifi, asks for the Pi's password one
     time, and puts this laptop's key on it - after that choice 5 connects
     without a password. It remembers the Pi in pi_address.txt.


  7  STOP THE CONSOLE

     Shuts the console down when you have finished with choice 1. While it
     is running there is a small black window on the taskbar - leave it
     alone, and use choice 7 rather than closing it by hand.


  8  QUIT

     Closes the menu. It does not stop the console - use 7 for that.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THINGS THE CONSOLE DOES THAT ARE NOT OBVIOUS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Ctrl+K            opens a search box that jumps to any page and runs any
                    command - stop, start a row, arm the pump, and so on.
  1 - 8             jump straight to a page.
  W A S D / arrows  drive it, while you are on the Live page, in Manual, and
                    only when the remote is off. Let go and it stops.
  Remote page       the whole rover from a phone: a thumb stick, the camera
                    turning left and right, and the arm. On a phone, the
                    Drive / Robotic arm tabs under the picture swap between
                    the stick and the arm.
  Manual / AI / Hold  the three buttons above the stick. Manual: you drive.
                    AI (auto): it follows the crop row by itself with the
                    camera and stops at each plant; the arm is locked and the
                    camera looks straight ahead. Hold: wheels locked still.
                    With no distance sensor fitted, AI mode asks once before
                    it starts and never goes faster than 30%. Stay beside it.
  Arm page          every joint with a slider, a live drawing of the arm, the
                    ready-made poses, and SAVE POSE: move the arm where you
                    want it, type a name, save - one tap brings it back. Saved
                    poses live on the Pi and survive new code being sent.
  SPACE             emergency stop.
  The sun / moon    switches between the dark console and a light one for
                    reading outdoors. It remembers which you chose.

  On the field map every state has its own SHAPE as well as its own colour -
  a circle is fine, a triangle wants looking at, a diamond was sprayed. Green
  and red are the one pair a red-green colourblind eye cannot separate, so
  the map is built not to need colour at all.

  If the websocket cannot get through your wifi, the page quietly falls back
  to polling instead of freezing on the last frame it received. The link chip
  at the top says "slow link" when that happens.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT ELSE IS IN HERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  rover_software\      The complete rover program. This is the same folder
                       you copy onto the Raspberry Pi - it is not a separate
                       or cut-down copy. The website lives inside it at
                       rover_software\ar750\web\.

  website_preview.html The saved picture of the console used by choice 3.

  rover_address.txt    Where the rover's address is remembered, for choice 2.

  pi_address.txt       Where the Pi's ssh address is remembered, for choices
                       5 and 6. Choice 6 writes it.

  tools\               The helpers AR-750.bat calls: starting everything
                       (start_rover.ps1, phone_link.html), sending the code
                       to the Pi (pi_deploy.bat), linking the laptop to it
                       (pi_login_once.bat, locate_pi.ps1), and waiting for
                       the console to be ready before the browser opens.
                       Their last run is logged in the same folder.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TWO HONEST NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  There is no trained AI model in here yet. Until you train one on your own
  plants, the rover photographs every plant and logs it as "not checked". It
  will not guess a disease it has not been taught. The guide at
  rover_software\docs\TRAINING_THE_MODEL.md walks through making one.

  The simulator is the real program with the hardware replaced. What you see
  on the dashboard is what the rover will do. What it cannot show you is
  whether your wiring is right - that is what the circuit diagram is for.
