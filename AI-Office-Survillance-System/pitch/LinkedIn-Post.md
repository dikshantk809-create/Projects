# WorkLens AI — LinkedIn Post (ready to copy)

## ✅ Main post (copy-paste this)

A few months ago, I could barely write a Python script.

This week, I watched an AI I built recognize people walking into an office — automatically, by their face. Still feels a little unreal. 👀

I've been quietly building something I call **WorkLens AI**.

The idea is simple: every office already has cameras — but they just record footage nobody ever watches. What if those same cameras could handle attendance and show how the day is actually going, with no new hardware?

So that's what I built. You point it at your existing webcam / IP / CCTV cameras, and it:

✅ Marks attendance automatically by face — no registers, no fingerprint machines
✅ Shows live who's working, idle, walking, or on their phone
✅ Runs all the office cameras together on a single PC (GPU)
✅ Creates clean Excel reports + an AI-written daily summary
✅ Opens on your phone, with a central dashboard for multiple offices

Honestly? It was not smooth.

I started out thinking I'd run it on a tiny Raspberry Pi — then learned the hard way why GPUs matter. I broke the video streams more times than I can count. I fought crashes I didn't understand, faces getting matched to the wrong names, and one bug that killed the entire app every time I switched a camera.

But every single problem taught me something — computer vision, real-time video, threading, and mostly just not quitting when the screen went black for the 50th time.

It's still early and there's a lot left to improve. But it works. And coming from someone who started as a complete beginner, that means a lot.

If you run an office (or know someone who does) and would let me pilot this, I'd genuinely love to talk. Feedback, ideas, even honest criticism — all welcome. 🙏

## 🎥 Tip for the video (since you're posting one)

- Keep it **30–60 seconds** — short wins on LinkedIn.
- Put the BEST moment in the **first 3 seconds**: faces being recognized live with name labels. People scroll fast.
- Suggested flow: live camera with names → the "who's doing what" panel updating → "Sab ON" showing many cameras at once → the Excel report / AI summary opening.
- Add **captions/text on screen** (most people watch on mute).
- Blur or get consent for any real faces shown — good practice, and it shows you take privacy seriously.
